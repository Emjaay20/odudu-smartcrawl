# The name Odudu

Odudu means "authority" in Ibibio, a language of Akwa Ibom, Nigeria.

An identity provider holds authority on behalf of the people who run it. It decides who someone is, what an app may do for them, and when that stops. The name is context for the product, and it is a reminder of whose authority it is: yours. You run Odudu, you keep its data, and you decide who signs in.
